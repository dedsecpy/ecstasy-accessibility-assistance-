# Assistance - Bangalore International Centre

## Assistance hours

Mon: 10:00-21:00, Tue: 10:00-21:00, Wed: 10:00-21:00, Thu: 10:00-21:00, Fri: 10:00-21:00, Sat: 10:00-21:00, Sun: 10:00-21:00. Phone +91 80 0000 0103.

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 24 hours ahead. Reception can arrange a wheelchair and an escort; no pre-booking needed.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is through the automatic main doors from the forecourt; reception is just inside.
