# Access notes - Bangalore International Centre

## Step-free route

Drop-off -> forecourt walkway -> gentle ramp -> automatic main doors -> lobby -> lift to every floor.

Step-free entry is through the automatic main doors from the forecourt; reception is just inside.

## Main entrance

Level automatic doors from the forecourt.

## Main doors (step-free)

Automatic main doors open 10:00-21:00.

## Reception help point

Reception help point by the doors, staffed during opening hours.

## Forecourt walkway

Smooth paved walkway, about 2 m wide, from the drop-off.

## Forecourt ramp

Gentle 1:20 ramp with handrails on both sides.

## Accessible toilet

Accessible toilets on every floor.

## Basement accessible parking

Two accessible bays in the basement next to the lift.

## Hearing loop

Induction loop in the auditorium tested and working.

## Seating and rest points

Lobby has ten chairs and benches by the gallery.
