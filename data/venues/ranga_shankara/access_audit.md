# Access notes - Ranga Shankara

## Step-free route

Front gate on 8th Cross -> open foyer -> auditorium ramp -> Auditorium (two wheelchair spaces in the front row).

Step-free entry is through the front gate on 8th Cross, which is open from 11:00 until the last show ends.

## Main entrance

Level entrance from 8th Cross through the front gate into the open foyer.

## Front gate (8th Cross)

Front gate open from 11:00 until the last show ends.

## Box office counter

Box office counter staffed whenever the gate is open.

## Foyer route

Foyer route to the auditorium is level and about 1.5 m wide.

## Auditorium ramp

Short ramp into the auditorium at about 1:10 for 4 m; handrail on one side only.

## Foyer benches

Benches in the open foyer seat about 20 people.

## Accessible toilet

Accessible toilet beside the foyer, right-hand transfer.

## Accessible parking

No on-site parking; drop-off at the gate. Street parking on 8th Cross fills by 19:00.

## Hearing loop

No information about a hearing loop in the auditorium.
