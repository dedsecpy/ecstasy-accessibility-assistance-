# Access notes - National Gallery of Modern Art

## Step-free route

Palace Road gate -> garden path (about 120 m) -> new wing entrance -> lift to the New Wing Galleries, or the link-bridge ramp into the mansion.

Step-free entry is via the new wing glass doors from the garden; the mansion's front steps have no ramp.

## Mansion entrance

The mansion entrance has seven steps and no ramp.

## New wing entrance

Level glass doors from the garden, open 10:00-17:00.

## New wing reception

New wing reception staffed during opening hours.

## Garden path

Garden path narrows to about 1.1 m between flower beds, with loose gravel in patches after rain.

## Link-bridge ramp

1:18 ramp across the link bridge into the mansion ground floor.

## Seating and rest points

Stone benches every 30-40 m along the garden path.

## Accessible toilet

Accessible toilet in the new wing lobby.

## Accessible parking

Four accessible bays inside the Palace Road gate, about 120 m from the new wing over gravel.

## Hearing loop

No information about a loop in the garden auditorium.
