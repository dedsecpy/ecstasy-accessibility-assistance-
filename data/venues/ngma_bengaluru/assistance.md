# Assistance - National Gallery of Modern Art

## Assistance hours

Mon: closed, Tue: 10:00-17:00, Wed: 10:00-17:00, Thu: 10:00-17:00, Fri: 10:00-17:00, Sat: 10:00-17:00, Sun: 10:00-17:00. Phone +91 80 0000 0105.

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 24 hours ahead. Two wheelchairs available at new wing reception.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is via the new wing glass doors from the garden; the mansion's front steps have no ramp.
