# Assistance - Visvesvaraya Industrial & Technological Museum

## Assistance hours

Mon: 09:30-18:00, Tue: 09:30-18:00, Wed: 09:30-18:00, Thu: 09:30-18:00, Fri: 09:30-18:00, Sat: 09:30-18:00, Sun: 09:30-18:00. Phone +91 80 0000 0104.

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 24 hours ahead. Wheelchairs to borrow at the ticket office, first come first served.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is via the permanent east ramp entrance beside the ticket office, open during museum hours.
