# Access notes - Visvesvaraya Industrial & Technological Museum

## Step-free route

Ticket office -> east-side ramp -> Engine Hall (ground floor) -> gallery lift to the Science Theatre and Space Gallery.

Step-free entry is via the permanent east ramp entrance beside the ticket office, open during museum hours.

## Main entrance

Three steps at the main entrance; a portable ramp is kept at security.

## East ramp entrance

East ramp entrance open during museum hours.

## Security desk

Security desk at the east entrance, staffed during opening hours.

## Front garden path

Paved garden path from the gate, about 1.8 m wide.

## East-side ramp

1:15 ramp with handrails on both sides.

## Accessible toilet

Accessible toilet on the ground floor only.

## Seating and rest points

Benches in every gallery.

## Accessible parking

Pay parking on Kasturba Road; two accessible bays are usually taken by 11:00.

## Hearing loop

No information about a loop in the Science Theatre.
