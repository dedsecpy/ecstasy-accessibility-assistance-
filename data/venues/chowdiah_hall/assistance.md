# Assistance - Chowdiah Memorial Hall

## Assistance hours

Mon: 10:00-20:00, Tue: 10:00-20:00, Wed: 10:00-20:00, Thu: 10:00-20:00, Fri: 10:00-20:00, Sat: 10:00-20:00, Sun: 10:00-18:00. Phone +91 80 0000 0101.

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 24 hours ahead. Box office staff help 10:00-20:00 daily (Sunday until 18:00). Pre-book 24 h ahead for an usher escort.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is only via Gate 2 on 16th Cross, which is kept shut. Ring the call bell; an usher opens it from 45 minutes before each concert.
