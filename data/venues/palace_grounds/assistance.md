# Assistance - Bangalore Palace Grounds

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 48 hours ahead. Book platform places 48 h ahead; stewards meet you at Gate 2.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is through the accessible lane at Gate 2 on Jayamahal Road, which opens with the event gates.
