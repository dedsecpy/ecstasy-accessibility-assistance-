# Access notes - Bangalore Palace Grounds

## Step-free route

Gate 2 accessible lane -> matting path over the lawn -> viewing platform ramp -> accessible viewing platform.

Step-free entry is through the accessible lane at Gate 2 on Jayamahal Road, which opens with the event gates.

## Main gate

Main gate has a raised kerb and ticket barriers with a step.

## Gate 2 accessible lane

Level accessible lane at Gate 2 opens with the event gates.

## Gate 2 help desk

Help desk at Gate 2 on event days.

## Matting path to the lawn

Temporary matting over grass, about 1.1 m wide with uneven joins; muddy after rain.

## Viewing platform ramp

Temporary ramp to the viewing platform at about 1:10, handrail on one side.

## Accessible parking

Accessible parking in the Gate 2 lot, about 350 m from the stage over gravel.

## Hearing loop

No hearing loop at outdoor events.
