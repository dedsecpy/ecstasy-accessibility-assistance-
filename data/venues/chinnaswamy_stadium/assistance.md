# Assistance - M. Chinnaswamy Stadium

## Assistance hours

Mon: 10:00-23:00, Tue: 10:00-23:00, Wed: 10:00-23:00, Thu: 10:00-23:00, Fri: 10:00-23:00, Sat: 10:00-23:00, Sun: 10:00-23:00. Phone +91 80 0000 0107.

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 48 hours ahead. Book accessible platform seats 48 h ahead; stewards escort from Gate 18.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is only via Gate 18 on Queen's Road, a wide accessible gate staffed by stewards from two hours before play.
