# Access notes - M. Chinnaswamy Stadium

## Step-free route

Queen's Road drop-off -> walkway -> Gate 18 -> ramp to the accessible viewing platform, or the pavilion lift to P1.

Step-free entry is only via Gate 18 on Queen's Road, a wide accessible gate staffed by stewards from two hours before play.

## Turnstile gates

Most turnstile gates have a step or narrow turnstiles.

## Gate 18 accessible entrance

Gate 18 accessible entrance staffed by stewards from two hours before play.

## Gate 18 steward desk

Steward desk at Gate 18 on match days.

## Queen's Road walkway

Crowded on match days; barricades leave about 1.2 m clear near Gate 18.

## Gate 18 ramp

1:15 ramp to the viewing platform, handrails on both sides.

## Seating and rest points

Accessible viewing platform has companion seats.

## Accessible toilet

Accessible toilet on the Gate 18 concourse.

## Hearing loop

No information about assistive listening.
