# Assistance desk

## Hours

The assistance desk is staffed Monday to Friday 10:00-17:00 and Saturday 10:00-14:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

For evening events the assistance desk is not staffed after 17:00 unless assistance has been pre-booked at least 48 hours in advance.

## Contact

Phone: 01234 567 890 (staffed hours only). Email: access@riversidehall.example.

## What the assistance desk can do

- Open the Side Gate on Mill Lane and meet visitors in the courtyard.
- Provide a portable ramp for the delivery entrance.
- Reserve seating in the Main Hall near the lift and at the end of a row.
- Reserve a seat in the ground floor foyer for visitors who need to rest.
- Confirm the current lift status by phone before you travel.

## Outside staffed hours

Security staff can open the Side Gate on request via the main entrance. Security staff are not trained to provide mobility assistance and must not lift or carry visitors.

## Pre-booking

We recommend pre-booking assistance 48 hours before your visit, especially for evening and weekend events.
