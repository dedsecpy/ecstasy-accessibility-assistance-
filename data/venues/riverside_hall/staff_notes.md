# Facilities staff notes

## 2026-03-14 — Side Gate intercom

Side Gate intercom replaced and tested. Rings through to the assistance desk during staffed hours. Outside staffed hours it is not answered.

## 2026-07-09 — Ramp handrail

Courtyard ramp handrail loose at the lower end. Section cordoned off. Ramp still usable but handrail should not be relied on.

## 2026-08-30 — Ramp handrail repaired

Handrail re-fixed. Cordon removed.

## 2026-09-20 — Courtyard building works

Scaffolding erected along the north wall of the courtyard for roof repairs, expected until mid-October 2026. The courtyard path between the Side Gate and the ramp is narrowed to approximately 800 mm at one point. Larger powered wheelchairs and mobility scooters may not pass. A portable ramp is available on request from the assistance desk to bypass the narrow section via the delivery entrance (staffed hours only). One of the two courtyard benches is inside the scaffolding zone and not available.
