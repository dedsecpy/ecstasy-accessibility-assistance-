# Access Audit — Riverside Hall

## Main entrance (Riverside Walk)

The main entrance has six stone steps with a single handrail on the left. There is no ramp and no platform lift at the main entrance. The main entrance is not step-free. The drop-off point on Riverside Walk serves only the main entrance.

## Step-free route (Side Gate, Mill Lane)

The only step-free route into the building is via the Side Gate on Mill Lane. The route is: Side Gate -> courtyard -> external ramp -> ground floor foyer.

The Side Gate is kept locked. There is an intercom at the Side Gate connected to the assistance desk. At the time of the audit the intercom was not working. Outside assistance desk hours the gate is opened by security staff on request.

## External ramp

The courtyard ramp is 14 metres long with a gradient of approximately 1:12. This is steeper than the recommended 1:20 for a ramp of this length and may be difficult for manual wheelchair users and people with limited stamina without assistance. There is a handrail on one side only. There are no resting places (level landings with seating) along the ramp.

## Lift

There is one passenger lift serving Ground, First and Second floors. Internal dimensions 1000 mm x 1250 mm, suitable for most manual and powered wheelchairs. The Main Hall is on the First floor. There is no alternative step-free route to the First floor; if the lift is out of service the Main Hall is not reachable without using stairs.

## Seating and rest points

Distance from the blue badge bays to the Side Gate is approximately 120 metres with no seating along the route. Two benches are located in the courtyard before the ramp. The ground floor foyer has four fixed chairs. Resting places are recommended at least every 50 metres on an access route; this is not currently met.

## Toilets

One accessible toilet on the Ground floor, right-hand transfer only. No accessible toilet on the First floor.

## Parking

Two blue badge bays on Mill Lane, approximately 120 metres from the Side Gate on a slight incline. No blue badge bays near the main entrance.

## Hearing loop

An induction loop is installed in the Main Hall. It was not tested during this audit.

## Recommendations (summary)

1. Update the public listing to describe the step-free route and its limitations rather than stating "wheelchair accessible".
2. Repair the Side Gate intercom and publish assistance desk hours.
3. Publish lift status and a contingency when the lift is out of service.
4. Add seating along the ramp and the route from the blue badge bays.
