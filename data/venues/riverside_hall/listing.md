# Riverside Hall — Venue Listing

Riverside Hall is a historic civic venue hosting lectures, fairs and community events.

## Accessibility

- Wheelchair accessible
- Lift to all floors
- Accessible toilet
- Step-free access
- Hearing loop in the Main Hall
- Assistance available

## Getting here

Main entrance on Riverside Walk. Blue badge parking available. Nearest bus stop: Riverside Walk (routes 12, 44).
