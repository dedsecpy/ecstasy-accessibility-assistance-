# Access notes - Lalbagh Glass House

## Step-free route

West Gate -> main avenue (about 500 m, or a battery buggy) -> Glass House ramp -> Glass House floor.

Step-free entry is through the West Gate pedestrian opening; battery buggies run from there to the Glass House.

## Main entrance

West Gate has a wide, level pedestrian opening.

## West Gate pedestrian entrance

West Gate pedestrian entrance open 06:00-19:00.

## Buggy help desk

Buggy help desk by the West Gate ticket counter, 09:00-17:00.

## Avenue to the Glass House

Paved avenue but crowded during the flower show; barricades leave about 1.2 m clear.

## Glass House ramp

Gentle ramp up to the Glass House floor.

## Accessible parking

Paid parking at the Double Road gate, about 400 m from the Glass House; drop-off allowed at the West Gate.
