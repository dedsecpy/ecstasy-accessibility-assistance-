# Assistance - Lalbagh Glass House

Mon: 09:00-17:00, Tue: 09:00-17:00, Wed: 09:00-17:00, Thu: 09:00-17:00, Fri: 09:00-17:00, Sat: 09:00-17:00, Sun: 09:00-17:00. Phone +91 80 0000 0106.

## Pre-booking

Pre-book assistance at least 24 hours ahead. Battery buggies run from the West Gate 09:00-17:00, first come first served.

## Getting in

Step-free entry is through the West Gate pedestrian opening; battery buggies run from there to the Glass House.
